# Code of conduct

We follow the spirit of the Contributor Covenant 2.1. Be respectful, assume good faith, keep discussion on the work, and welcome people of every background and experience level. Harassment, personal attacks, and discriminatory language are not tolerated. Maintainers may remove comments, issues or contributors that break these rules. Report problems privately to the maintainers through the repository's Security tab contact option or the email listed in the repository profile.
